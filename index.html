Seniors

Beautiful Minds Social Center is a cost efficient alternative or supplemental program to home healthcare, assisted living and nursing homes. Our center enables continued community-based living for individuals with physical and cognitive limitations and provides respite for their caregivers. We also offer an upbeat environment with fun filled activities for those active seniors that just want a place to socialize and stay active with their mind and bodies. Our program and activities are designed to include all skill levels that will enrich the mind and body of all participants. At Beautiful Minds we believe that individuals are unique, therefore our staff takes the time to get to know all of our members as we assist them in doing what they love.
