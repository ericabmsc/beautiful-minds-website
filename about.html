About
Beautiful Minds

Beautiful Minds Social Center is a cost efficient alternative or supplemental program to home health care, moving your loved one to an assisted living facility or a nursing home far before needed. Our center enables continued community-based living for individuals with physical and cognitive limitations and provides respite for their caregivers. We also offer an upbeat environment with fun filled activities for those active seniors that just want a place to socialize and stay active with their mind and bodies. Our program and activities are designed to include all skill levels that will enrich the mind and body of all participants. At Beautiful Minds we believe that individuals are unique, therefore our staff takes the time to get to know all of our members as we assist them in doing what they love.

Our Mission at Beautiful Minds Social Center is to provide day services to meet the needs of our loved ones as an affordable and effective alternative to institutionalization. We Will maximize the quality of life with a professionalized and kindness as we provide a combination of mental and physical activities appropriated to individual’s needs.

Why

Beautiful Minds is dedicated to my Grandmother, Eva Miskinis. It is because of our family’s past personal challenges within senior care that I have pursued my passion to make this process and easy one for families. I wanted to make sure that to create a fun socialization center where seniors can begin living out their Golden Years.
